# Garlic Herb Chicken with Sautéed Spinach

This dish is simple, quick, and bursting with flavor. The lemon brightens up the entire meal, making it feel fresh and light.

## Ingredients:
*   2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
*   2 tablespoons olive oil, divided
*   2 cloves garlic, minced
*   5 oz fresh spinach
*   1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
*   1 teaspoon dried oregano (or 1 tablespoon fresh)
*   1/2 teaspoon dried thyme (or 1/2 tablespoon fresh)
*   Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

## Instructions:
1.  **Prepare Chicken:** Pat chicken breasts dry with a paper towel. Season generously with salt, black pepper, oregano, and thyme on both sides.
2.  **Cook Chicken:** Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken breasts and cook for 5-7 minutes per side, or until golden brown and cooked through (internal temperature reaches 165°F/74°C). Remove chicken from the skillet and set aside on a plate, tented with foil, to rest.
3.  **Sauté Spinach:** In the same skillet, add the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil. Add minced garlic and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant. Add the fresh spinach to the skillet in batches if necessary, stirring until wilted, about 2-3 minutes.
4.  **Finish and Serve:** Stir in the fresh lemon juice with the spinach. Season the spinach with a pinch of salt and pepper if needed. Slice the cooked chicken breasts and serve immediately alongside the sautéed spinach.
